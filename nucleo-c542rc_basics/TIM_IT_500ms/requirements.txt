[实验名称] 定时器周期中断实验
[功能目标] 使用 STM32C542RCT6 的 TIM6 实现板载 LED（PA5）周期性闪烁，LED 每 500ms 切换一次状态。
[开发板和MCU型号] NUCLEO-C542RC 开发板，搭载 STM32C542RCT6 MCU

[Experiment Name] Timer Periodic Interrupt Experiment
[Objective] Use TIM6 of the STM32C542RCT6 to make the onboard LED (PA5) blink periodically, toggling its state every 500 ms.
[Board and MCU] NUCLEO-C542RC development board, equipped with theSTM32C542RCT6 MCU.
