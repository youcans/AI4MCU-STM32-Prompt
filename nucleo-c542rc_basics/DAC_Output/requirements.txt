[实验名称] DAC 模拟量信号输出
[开发板和MCU型号] NUCLEO-C542RC 开发板，搭载 STM32C542RCT6 MCU
[功能目标] 使用 STM32C542RCT6 的 DAC1 通道1 经 PA4 引脚输出模拟电压：周期性更新 DAC 转换值以生成锯齿波，并通过 PA5 引脚指示锯齿波的输出周期。

[Experiment Name] DAC Analog Signal Output
[Board and MCU] NUCLEO-C542RC development board, equipped with the STM32C542RCT6 MCU.
[Objective] Use DAC1 Channel 1 of the STM32C542RCT6 to output an analog voltage via the PA4 pin: periodically update the DAC conversion value to generate a sawtooth wave, and indicate the output period of the sawtooth wave via the PA5 pin.
