[实验名称] GPIO输出翻转实验（LED闪烁）
[功能目标] 使用 STM32G431RBT6 的 PA5 引脚，每隔 500 ms 切换一次板载 LED 的状态，实现 LED 闪烁。
[开发板和MCU型号] NUCLEO-G431RB开发板，搭载 STM32G431RBT6 MCU。

[Experiment Name] GPIO Output Toggle Experiment (LED Blinking)
[Objective] Use the PA5 pin of the STM32G431RBT6 to toggle the state of the onboard LED every 500 ms, achieving LED blinking.
[Board and MCU] NUCLEO-G431RB development board, equipped with the STM32G431RBT6 MCU.
